## Quick answer

DJI's public end-of-service notice groups four products under a September 1, 2026 service-suspension date:

1. Datalink 3;
2. Zenmuse Z30;
3. Zenmuse XT S; and
4. Manifold 2.

DJI states that from the suspension date it will no longer provide services involving product inquiries, technical support and product maintenance for the listed products. The notice does not itself establish a flight ban, a future firmware schedule, every regional warranty result, third-party parts availability or a resale-value change.

## Evidence boundary

This record separates the service-suspension notice from consequences that require other evidence.

The notice confirms:

- the four product names;
- their production-suspension months;
- the September 1, 2026 service-suspension date; and
- the end of DJI services involving product inquiries, technical support and product maintenance after that date.

The notice does not separately establish:

- whether an installed unit is legal or safe to operate;
- a product-by-product future firmware policy;
- every regional warranty contract;
- remaining manufacturer or third-party parts inventory;
- independent repair availability;
- a depreciation percentage; or
- a mandatory replacement model.

Reboot Hub has not independently verified every regional service workflow or third-party parts inventory.

## Reboot Hub Legacy Enterprise Support Lifecycle Gate

1. **Identify the exact asset.** Record the product name, variant, serial number, installed role and connected aircraft, controller, payload or computer.
2. **Map mission dependency.** Separate spare or training hardware from equipment required for inspection, thermal response, command links, payload control or onboard computing.
3. **Preserve the working baseline.** Record firmware and app versions, configuration, manuals, calibration state, known limits and a repeatable functional test.
4. **Document maintenance condition.** Capture symptoms, inspection findings, service history, connectors, mounts, cables and environmental exposure.
5. **Verify support and parts evidence.** Retain dated official records and current regional terms. Treat unverified supplier inventory as unknown.
6. **Validate a replacement in the real workflow.** Test payload compatibility, data output, control path, software, mounting, power, training and regulatory requirements.
7. **Record the disposition.** Continue, restrict, redeploy, hold for spares or retire the unit based on mission criticality and verified evidence, with a defined review date.
