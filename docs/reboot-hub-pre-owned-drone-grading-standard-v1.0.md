# Reboot Hub Pre-Owned Drone Grading Standard

Version 1.0 | Reviewed July 2026

Canonical source: https://reboot-hub.com/pages/drone-grading-standard

## Purpose and scope

This document defines the condition labels Reboot Hub uses for its own pre-owned drone listings. It is a seller-specific disclosure framework, not an industry-wide certification, a manufacturer standard, or a substitute for the exact product listing.

The standard documents two labels: A+ / Flawless and A / Pristine Pre-Owned. A grade describes the inspected aircraft at the time of assessment. It does not automatically describe every battery, controller, accessory, service entitlement, warranty term, activation status, or regional compatibility item in the bundle. Those facts must be disclosed separately.

## Quick reference

### A+ / Flawless

- No visible cosmetic marks under the defined inspection conditions.
- No observed crack, chip, deformation, exposed cable, binding mechanism, or other material physical defect.
- Core functional checks are completed without an unresolved warning.
- Activation status is disclosed separately and is not inferred from the grade.
- Battery, controller, accessories, warranty, and service eligibility are disclosed separately.

### A / Pristine Pre-Owned

- Light cosmetic wear may be present and must be visible in the exact-unit listing.
- No observed crack, chip, structural deformation, or hidden cosmetic restoration is represented as original condition.
- Core functional checks are completed without an unresolved warning.
- Any known replaced part, repair history, or uncertainty about originality is disclosed rather than hidden behind the grade.
- Battery, controller, accessories, warranty, and service eligibility are disclosed separately.

## Evidence domains

### 1. Identity and listing match

Confirm the model and configuration. Keep the serial number and internal record associated with the exact unit, while redacting sensitive identifiers from public documents. The listing photos and bundle description must correspond to the unit being sold.

### 2. Cosmetic condition

Inspect the airframe, arms, landing surfaces, camera housing, gimbal area, visible fasteners, ports, controller, and included accessories under consistent lighting. Photographs should show any wear that could influence a reasonable buyer's decision.

### 3. Structure and propulsion

Inspect for cracks, chips, deformation, looseness, impact marks, abnormal movement, damaged propellers, and other visible signs that require further diagnosis. A cosmetic grade cannot override a structural or flight-safety concern.

### 4. Battery and power

Record the battery identity and any accessible health, cycle, warning, swelling, terminal, charging, or power-on evidence. Cycle count alone is not proof of battery health. Batteries are assessed and disclosed separately from the aircraft cosmetic grade.

### 5. Imaging and gimbal

Check camera output, image stability, gimbal startup and controllable movement, and visible lens or sensor-window condition. Calibration is not used to conceal binding, impact deformation, abnormal noise, or a repeatable warning.

### 6. Control, communication, sensors, and software

Confirm controller pairing and normal startup behavior for the supplied configuration. Record the exact app warning, firmware state, sensor warning, or account-binding issue when present. Menu wording and available checks vary by model, controller, app, firmware, and region.

## Grade decision rules

1. Safety and function take precedence over appearance. A visually clean unit with an unresolved structural, battery, propulsion, gimbal, camera, sensor, controller, or account-binding concern does not qualify for A+ or A under this document.
2. The exact-unit listing controls. Generic product photographs or a grade name cannot replace photographs and disclosures for the unit offered.
3. Unknown is not the same as passed. If activation, repair history, liquid exposure, originality, battery status, or service eligibility cannot be verified, the uncertainty must be stated.
4. Originality claims require evidence. A unit may be described as original or unmodified only when inspection and available history support that statement.
5. Bundle components are graded separately. Aircraft, battery, controller, charger, propellers, and accessories may have different histories and condition.
6. Warranty and service eligibility are separate. The current listing and policy control Reboot Hub warranty terms. DJI Care or manufacturer service eligibility depends on the applicable provider's current rules and is not created by a Reboot Hub grade.
7. Material changes require reassessment. A return, impact, repair, component swap, battery change, firmware event, or new warning can invalidate an earlier grade.

## Minimum assessment record

The internal assessment record should retain, where applicable:

- assessment date and reviewer;
- exact model and configuration;
- internal serial or unit identifier;
- exact-unit photographs;
- cosmetic observations and disclosed wear;
- structural and propulsion observations;
- battery identity and accessible health evidence;
- startup, app, sensor, gimbal, camera, and controller observations;
- known repair, replacement, activation, or region information;
- included accessories and bundle differences;
- unresolved warnings, unknowns, and exclusions;
- final grade and the reason it was assigned.

## Buyer verification checklist

Before relying on a grade, a buyer should be able to verify:

- that the photographs show the exact unit;
- what cosmetic wear is present;
- whether any crack, deformation, exposed cable, binding, or warning exists;
- which battery and controller are included;
- what battery evidence is available;
- whether the unit starts, pairs, and produces normal camera output;
- what activation, account-binding, region, repair, and replacement history is known;
- which accessories are included;
- which warranty and return terms apply to that listing.

## Evidence boundaries and limitations

- A non-invasive inspection cannot prove the absence of every latent or intermittent fault.
- A clean exterior cannot prove that a unit has never been opened, exposed to liquid, overheated, crashed, or repaired when records are unavailable.
- Battery cycle count, voltage, or app status alone does not guarantee future battery performance.
- Firmware, app menus, service eligibility, account binding, and regional compatibility can change after publication.
- This standard does not authorize flight. The operator remains responsible for legal, environmental, airworthiness, and pre-flight decisions.
- The grade applies only to the recorded assessment state and exact bundle described in the listing.
